A few helpful things

Good to know.

How do we check if our date is available?+

Send an enquiry with your date and celebration details. The photographer will confirm availability with you directly; submitting this form does not reserve a date.

When will we receive our photographs?+

Editing timelines vary with the season and the collection. Your photographer will confirm the expected delivery schedule when you discuss your booking.

Do you travel outside Nairobi?+

Travel across Kenya can be discussed. Share your venue or location in the enquiry, and the photographer can confirm availability and any travel costs.

When is our booking officially confirmed?+

An enquiry is only a first conversation. Your date is confirmed only after the photographer has agreed with you directly and any required agreement and retainer are complete.
